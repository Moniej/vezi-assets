## H-A — Earnings Surprise × Post-Announcement Drift

1. **Information hypothesis**: NGX stocks underreact to material earnings surprises, producing drift beyond the announcement window.
2. **Economic mechanism**: investors anchor on the pre-announcement trend and adjust slowly to new information, producing serial correlation in returns following an earnings surprise (classic PEAD).
3. **Available data sources**: news-derived `extracted_facts` (revenue/net_profit/ebit/ebitda, already FSI-extracted from Nairametrics/MarketForces articles, Stages 11–12) and the platform's pre-existing filing-based earnings calendar (`data/staging/xissuer/earnings_calendar.csv`, used by H-006).
4. **Source feasibility**: both news outlets already cleared (Stage 10A/10B); the filing calendar is internal, already ingested, no new access needed.
5. **Historical coverage**: **already directly tested once** — H-006 (PEAD via filing-window market-reaction proxy) ran the identical economic hypothesis on the filing calendar and was **rejected** (`docs/PREREG_H-006.md`, ledger: `H-006 rejected`). This is not a new hypothesis in economic substance; it is the same PEAD claim on a different information channel (news-derived structured facts instead of filing timestamps).
6. **Ticker coverage across the H-011 universe**: live query — of H-011's 20 names, **9** have at least one news-derived revenue/profit/EBIT/EBITDA fact (CAVERTON, CILEASING, CUTIX, LASACO, MCNICHOLS, NCR, PRESTIGE, REDSTAREX, UNIVINSURE, VERITASKAP — 10 actually, one fact each mostly). Of those, **only CAVERTON has more than one observation of the same metric** (2 revenue facts).
7. **Historical depth**: CAVERTON's 2 revenue observations are **not a comparable period pair** — one is FY2024 full-year (₦40.1bn), the other is H1 2026 half-year (₦14.68bn). A surprise/change calculation requires like-for-like periods; this pair doesn't qualify without further normalization work not yet done. Every other H-011 ticker has exactly **one** observation — mathematically insufficient to compute "magnitude and direction of change vs. prior comparable period" at all.
8. **Novelty versus existing database**: the underlying figures are already known-novel per Stage 11/12's own audit (news reporting NGX-filed but previously un-extracted figures) — but the *surprise construct* (actual vs. expectation/prior) cannot currently be computed for any H-011 name.
9. **PIT safety**: unresolved and **already flagged as the specific reason earnings content was excluded from H-019** (Stage 12 §12F/§12H: unaudited/interim news-reported figures carry an unresolved audited-vs-reported figure-integrity risk; ~41% of the broader novel-event corpus was found PIT-uncertain on this exact dimension).
10. **Provenance quality**: high for the individual facts (100% grounding pass rate, Stage 11/12) — but provenance of a *fact* is not the same as PIT-safety of a *surprise variable*, which additionally requires knowing what the market's prior expectation was, which this platform does not capture at all (no analyst-estimate or consensus data source exists on this platform).
11. **Deduplication requirements**: same event_pipeline/document mechanism as H-019; not the binding constraint here.
12. **FSI extraction feasibility**: proven working (Stage 11/12, 100% grounding) for the raw facts; extracting "earnings quality / unusual items" specifically has **never been attempted** — `configs/fact_taxonomy.toml`'s `[operating_performance]` category has `margin_change`/`guidance` leaves but no "unusual item" or "one-off charge" leaf, meaning this would require a taxonomy extension before any extraction could be attempted.
13. **Potential signal definition**: (not constructed — blocked at input stage) would be surprise-sign × magnitude, decayed over a fixed post-announcement window, mirroring H-006's own (rejected) construction.
14. **Potential confounds**: identical to H-006's own disclosed confounds (illiquidity-driven return staleness masquerading as drift) plus the same size/distress confound flagged for H-019 (§14G) — the same small-cap names are involved.
15. **Liquidity/execution considerations**: same capacity constraint as every H-011-adjacent hypothesis on this platform — this universe is capacity-constrained by construction.
16. **Expected observation count**: **effectively zero currently constructible** — a "surprise" needs ≥2 comparable-period observations per ticker, which exists for 0 of 20 H-011 names today.
17. **Economically material?**: yes, in principle — earnings are the canonical material-information category. The blocker is entirely data-availability, not materiality.
18. **Plausibly underprocessed by NGX?**: cannot be assessed — no surprise variable exists to test underreaction against.
19. **Independent of H-011/H-019?**: the underlying fact type (earnings) is explicitly the category H-019 excluded; testing it under a different construction (surprise/drift vs. H-019's discrete-event presence) is a different economic claim, but shares the identical PIT weakness H-019 already declined to accept.
20. **Decision: NO-GO, as currently specified.** Not because the hypothesis is uninteresting, but because (a) it is the same economic claim H-006 already tested and rejected on a cleaner data channel, (b) the required surprise-construction input doesn't exist for any H-011 name in usable form, and (c) the exact PIT risk that excluded earnings from H-019 is unresolved here too. Revisit only if a genuine period-matched, multi-observation earnings series is built for a meaningful share of the universe — which would be a new, separately-scoped data-acquisition project, not a signal-construction one.

## H-B — Regulatory Event × Liquidity/Attention

1. **Information hypothesis**: regulatory/compliance disclosures produce persistent repricing, most detectably in thinly-followed names.
2. **Economic mechanism**: regulatory actions carry hard, verifiable information (unlike qualitative news) that the market may be slow to fully price, especially for names with low analyst/media attention.
3. **Available data sources**: `events` table, `event_type='regulatory_action'` (already a taxonomy leaf); Nairametrics/MarketForces, both cleared.
4. **Source feasibility**: identical to H-019's — no new access issue.
5. **Historical coverage**: live query — **3 regulatory_action events exist in the entire H-011 universe**: REGALINS + UNIVINSURE (both the *same* underlying NGX suspension announcement, 2025-09-01, over unfiled audited statements) and TANTALIZER (a closed-period trading-rule cautionary letter, 2026-02-24). That is **2 independent real-world regulatory storylines**, not 3.
6. **Ticker coverage across the H-011 universe**: 3 of 20 names (15%) — but really 2 of 20 (10%) counting independent events, not rows.
7. **Historical depth**: both events fall within a single ~5-month window (Sept 2025–Feb 2026); no multi-year regulatory event history exists for this universe in the archive.
8. **Novelty versus existing database**: both were already confirmed NOVEL in Stage 11's audit; nothing new found this stage.
9. **PIT safety**: structurally the cleanest of the five tracks — regulatory actions are discrete, dateable, non-figure-based disclosures (same "no unaudited-figure risk" property that made H-019's GMC/CIR families PIT-safe).
10. **Provenance quality**: both existing events are fully document-linked (confirmed, part of the 18/18 clean set from the Stage 14 provenance remediation).
11. **Deduplication requirements**: REGALINS/UNIVINSURE is the textbook case Stage 10E's ticker-identity fix was built to handle correctly (same date, same event_type, different ticker) — already regression-tested, not new work.
12. **FSI extraction feasibility**: proven (these 3 rows were extracted and ingested via the existing pipeline already).
13. **Potential signal definition**: **cannot yet be defined** — the existing `direction='bearish'` values on these 3 rows were set ad hoc at ingestion time, not derived from any frozen, deterministic rule the way H-019's §14C direction table works. Building an H-B-equivalent to §14C (e.g., "suspension for non-filing → bearish; routine cautionary letter with no suspension → unknown; regulatory *approval* → ?") is unstarted work, explicitly required before any signal exists, per the standing rule against using ad hoc/ungoverned direction labels.
14. **Potential confounds**: attention/coverage itself may correlate with the event (a suspension generates news precisely *because* it's unusual for a quiet name) — a selection-effect confound worth naming now, before any data is gathered further.
15. **Liquidity/execution considerations**: a *trading suspension* event specifically makes the affected position **untradeable during the suspension window by definition** — this is a first-order execution constraint unique to this track, not shared by H-A/C/D/E, and must be designed into any future signal (e.g., can only enter/exit around the suspension, not during it).
16. **Expected observation count**: 2 independent events currently — even smaller than H-019's starting n=6.
17. **Economically material?**: yes, unambiguously — a trading suspension is about as material as NGX information gets.
18. **Plausibly underprocessed by NGX?**: plausible in principle (small, illiquid names, exactly H-011's universe) but entirely untested — no direction rule exists yet to even check.
19. **Independent of H-011/H-019?**: yes in construction — H-019 explicitly excluded `regulatory_action` from its scope (Stage 14 §14I), so this track doesn't overlap H-019's dataset, and regulatory content has no relationship to H-011's size-ranking inputs.
20. **Decision: CONDITIONAL.** The information type is real, material, PIT-clean, and genuinely unused by either existing hypothesis — the strongest structural case of the five on paper. It is blocked entirely on *volume*: 2 independent events is not a research base. The next step, if authorized, is bounded and well-defined (unlike H-D below): search all 20 H-011 names plus a modest expansion beyond H-011 for regulatory_action-type disclosures (suspensions, sanctions, license actions, approvals), build a frozen direction rule mirroring §14C's discipline, and re-assess coverage — a Stage-11-shaped pilot, not a new architecture.

## H-C — Corporate Action Economic Impact × Market Reaction

1. **Information hypothesis**: corporate actions that materially change a company's cash-flow, risk, or capital structure produce a market reaction distinguishable from routine announcements.
2. **Economic mechanism**: the market may misprice the *magnitude* of a structural change even when it correctly identifies the *direction* — e.g., correctly pricing "this is a capital raise" but not correctly pricing the resulting dilution/leverage change.
3. **Available data sources**: `events` (`capital_raise`, `debt_issuance`, `debt_default_or_restructuring`, `major_contract`, `asset_disposal`, `merger`, `ownership_change` — all pre-existing taxonomy leaves), news outlets already cleared.
4. **Source feasibility**: no new issue.
5. **Historical coverage**: live query — **4 `capital_raise` events exist in the H-011 universe** (RTBRISCOE, REGALINS, LASACO, SUNUASSUR — all rights-issue/private-placement actions). **Zero** `debt_issuance`, `debt_default_or_restructuring`, `major_contract`, or `asset_disposal` events exist for any H-011 name. `merger`/`ownership_change`/`corporate_restructuring` are already claimed by H-019 (2 and 2 events respectively, already frozen there).
6. **Ticker coverage**: 4 of 20 (20%) for capital_raise; 0 of 20 for every other candidate class H-C names.
7. **Historical depth**: all 4 capital_raise events cluster in a 5-month window (Feb–Aug 2026) — no multi-year depth.
8. **Novelty versus existing database**: already confirmed NOVEL for these 4 (Stage 11/13). The economic-mechanism content H-C wants (rights-issue structure, share counts, pricing, ratio) is **already captured in these rows' own `notes` fields** from Stage 11/12's ingestion — e.g. LASACO's "9,236,321,546 shares at N2.00, 5-for-6 ratio" — this is genuinely richer than a bare direction label already, without further FSI work.
9. **PIT safety**: same as H-B — discrete, non-figure-reliant disclosures, structurally clean.
10. **Provenance quality**: all 4 already document-linked, clean.
11. **Deduplication requirements**: none currently binding (no ticker has 2 capital_raise events).
12. **FSI extraction feasibility**: proven for basic structured facts; the deeper "economic mechanism chain" the instruction asks for (event → what changed → which cash-flow/risk/capital variable → expected persistence) has **never been attempted** on this platform — it would require a new, disclosed extraction schema beyond what `extracted_facts`/`events` currently model, though it could plausibly reuse the existing `causal_chain_steps` table (already built for the FSI reasoning engine's Step 1-13 draft pass, currently unused for anything except the fact-level reasoning chain in `investment_implications`) rather than requiring a new table.
13. **Potential signal definition**: not constructed. The most obvious construct — dilution magnitude (new shares ÷ existing shares) — is directly computable from the 4 rows' own already-captured `notes` content without new extraction, which is a notably lower-effort starting point than H-A or H-D.
14. **Potential confounds**: **this is exactly the track's own most serious, already-disclosed objection** — every one of these 4 events is a rights issue/private placement that a completed primary-filing extraction campaign would find *directly* from the NGX corporate-action filing, with no news intermediary needed at all. This is the identical "largely a faster/cheaper path to filing-derivable information, not exclusive information" critique Stage 11 §11H and Stage 13 §13F already raised for the recapitalization-capital-actions family — which is *why* it was excluded from H-019 in the first place. H-C inherits this objection in full; it does not resolve it merely by asking the FSI to extract mechanism instead of direction.
15. **Liquidity/execution considerations**: dilution events mechanically change share count and float — any signal here needs to account for the corporate action's own effect on subsequent ADTV/liquidity metrics, a real modeling complexity not present in H-019.
16. **Expected observation count**: 4 currently, all one event type; the richer classes (debt restructuring, major contracts, disposals) have **zero** current coverage and would need a fresh, bounded search — same shape of work as the Stage 15 expansion that took H-019 from 6 to 11 events.
17. **Economically material?**: yes for the capital-structure classes; "major contract" specifically needs a materiality filter (the taxonomy leaf exists but nothing currently distinguishes a genuinely major contract from a routine commercial announcement — the instruction's own explicit concern, unaddressed).
18. **Plausibly underprocessed by NGX?**: weak case, precisely because of the redundancy objection in #14 — if the information is easily filing-derivable, the market plausibly already has it via the primary disclosure, independent of any news intermediary.
19. **Independent of H-011/H-019?**: **the most compromised of the five on this specific dimension** — H-C's strongest currently-covered event class (`capital_raise`) is the exact family Stage 13/14 already excluded from H-019 for redundancy reasons, and H-C's other qualifying event types (`merger`, `ownership_change`, `corporate_restructuring`) are the families H-019 already owns. A "new" H-C hypothesis risks being either redundant with primary filings (capital_raise) or literally overlapping H-019's own frozen dataset (the other types) unless scoped very carefully to genuinely distinct event types (debt restructuring, major contracts, disposals) that currently have zero coverage.
20. **Decision: NO-GO on the currently-covered event classes (capital_raise — inherits H-019's own redundancy exclusion); CONDITIONAL only on the *uncovered* classes (debt restructuring, major contracts, asset disposals), which would require a fresh, bounded search effort with no existing seed data at all — the least-started track of the five in terms of actual usable coverage, despite the taxonomy already supporting it.**

## H-D — Information Diffusion Speed × Subsequent Returns

1. **Information hypothesis**: NGX information propagates slowly enough across outlets/market attention that diffusion speed itself predicts subsequent repricing magnitude/persistence.
2. **Economic mechanism**: if outlet A publishes materially before outlet B, and the market's full reaction only completes once broader coverage (outlet B, wider attention) arrives, then the A→B lag is itself a tradable proxy for "how much of the reaction is still ahead."
3. **Available data sources**: same two outlets as every other track.
4. **Source feasibility**: no new access issue, but the *research design itself* requires something neither outlet's current usage has ever produced (see #5).
5. **Historical coverage**: live query — **every single ticker-scoped news event or document ever ingested in this database's history (18 of 18) traces to source_id=16, a single placeholder registration covering both approved outlets.** Confirmed directly: zero genuine cross-outlet duplicate pairs (the same real-world event, captured from *both* Nairametrics and MarketForces, with two distinct, comparable publication timestamps) have ever been captured on this platform. Stage 13's broader search-level audit found MarketForces contributing only ~5% of processed articles in this corpus (Stage 12 §12D) — a structural under-sampling of the second outlet, not a finding that it doesn't exist.
6. **Ticker coverage**: not meaningfully assessable — the unit of analysis (a matched cross-outlet pair) has never existed.
7. **Historical depth**: zero — there is no historical series of diffusion-lag observations to describe.
8. **Novelty versus existing database**: the *concept* is fully novel (nothing like it has been attempted); but novelty of a concept with zero supporting data is not the same as a research-ready track.
9. **PIT safety**: cannot be assessed in the abstract — would depend entirely on whether both outlets' publication timestamps can be resolved to genuinely different, reliable moments, which (per Stage 13 §13E) this corpus has never achieved better than date-level precision for even a *single* outlet, let alone a reliable inter-outlet lag measured in less than a day.
10. **Provenance quality**: not applicable yet — no matched pairs exist to assess.
11. **Deduplication requirements**: this is the track's own central object of study (distinguishing first publication from republication/corroboration) rather than a preliminary step to get past — meaning the "deduplication requirement" and "the actual research question" are the same problem, which is unusual and worth naming explicitly.
12. **FSI extraction feasibility**: the FSI can extract publication dates from individual articles (proven, Stages 11-14) — it cannot, by itself, solve the harder problem of *confirming* that two independently-fetched articles describe the *same* underlying event with enough confidence to compute a lag between them; §14D's own disclosed "identity content agreement is a human/LLM judgment point, not mechanical" finding applies here with even higher stakes, since an identity-matching error directly corrupts the lag measurement itself.
13. **Potential signal definition**: cannot be responsibly proposed — no observation exists to derive one from, and inventing a plausible-sounding definition without data would be exactly the "manufacture directional variance" the standing rules prohibit, applied to a research design instead of a direction label.
14. **Potential confounds**: (anticipated, not tested) an outlet's own publication cadence/staffing may correlate with company size/attention independent of any genuine "diffusion" effect — i.e., a size/attention confound structurally similar to the one flagged for H-019 (§14G), likely worse here since outlet choice may itself correlate with company prominence.
15. **Liquidity/execution considerations**: not assessable without data.
16. **Expected observation count**: **zero, currently, and not obviously fixable by more processing of the existing corpus** — this requires a fundamentally different acquisition strategy (deliberately searching for pairs, not just more articles) that hasn't been attempted.
17. **Economically material?**: the concept (diffusion speed) is itself the informational content, not a specific fact — materiality would need to be assessed per underlying event, which doesn't exist yet.
18. **Plausibly underprocessed by NGX?**: this track's entire premise is a specific, testable claim about underprocessing — but the platform currently has no way to test it.
19. **Independent of H-011/H-019?**: yes in concept, trivially — neither existing hypothesis touches multi-outlet timing at all.
20. **Decision: NO-GO, not for lack of merit but for lack of a feasible starting point.** This is the only one of the five tracks where the *research design itself*, not merely coverage depth, is currently unimplementable — there is no existing seed data, and manufacturing a synthetic proxy would violate the standing rules against inventing data. A future attempt would need to be a dedicated, narrowly-scoped pilot explicitly built to *find* matched cross-outlet pairs (not simply process more single-outlet articles), which is materially different work from anything Stages 10–15 have done, and should be proposed as its own separately-authorized effort rather than folded into a general five-track sweep.

## H-E — Event × Market Microstructure State

1. **Information hypothesis**: the market's reaction to an information event depends on the microstructure state (liquidity, attention, momentum) the event arrives into — not just the event's own content.
2. **Economic mechanism**: illiquid, low-attention, or unusually quiet names may show larger or more persistent reactions to the same information than liquid, well-covered names, because less price discovery has already occurred.
3. **Available data sources**: `equity_prices` (close, volume, `value_traded`) — confirmed live: **320 distinct tickers, 2014-06-30 to 2026-07-21**, genuinely deep and broad; `index_levels` for market-wide context; no new source needed — this track needs no news/FSI acquisition of its own, only event data from another track to interact with.
4. **Source feasibility**: fully internal, already-ingested platform data — the only track of the five with zero external-access dependency.
5. **Historical coverage**: excellent for the *state variables themselves* (12+ years, broad universe); **entirely dependent on whichever event track supplies the "event" side of the interaction** — and every other track (H-A, H-B, H-C, H-D) is currently thin-to-empty for H-011 names, as documented above.
6. **Ticker coverage across H-011 universe**: full price/volume coverage for all 20 names (confirmed via the same `equity_prices` table used throughout H-011/H-019's own construction) — the state-variable side is not the bottleneck.
7. **Historical depth**: genuinely deep (12+ years) for state variables; bounded by whichever event series it's paired with for the interaction itself.
8. **Novelty versus existing database**: the *state variables* are not novel (H-011's own `size_scores()`, the ADTV liquidity cap, and H-016's liquidity-factor work all already use closely related quantities) — the *novelty* here is entirely in the interaction-testing design, not the inputs.
9. **PIT safety**: the state variables themselves are already PIT-disciplined throughout this platform (`equity_prices_asof`, `index_levels_asof`, vintage/confidence-pinned reads) — this track inherits that discipline directly rather than needing to establish it fresh, a real advantage over H-A/B/C/D.
10. **Provenance quality**: n/a for the state-variable side (platform market data, not news-derived); inherits whichever event track it's paired with for the event side.
11. **Deduplication requirements**: none of its own — inherits from the paired event track.
12. **FSI extraction feasibility**: not needed for the state-variable side at all; this is the one track that doesn't stress-test the FSI, which is worth noting since the instruction frames FSI use as central to the program overall — H-E's real dependency is on `backtest_xs.py`-style panel machinery, already proven.
13. **Potential signal definition**: an *interaction term* (event × liquidity-decile, event × turnover-z-score, event × prior-momentum), not a standalone signal — genuinely cannot be specified further until a host event series exists.
14. **Potential confounds**: interaction effects are notoriously prone to overfitting/spurious discovery with small event counts (exactly H-A through H-D's shared problem) — H-E would inherit and *compound* whichever host track's sample-size weakness it's paired with, since splitting an already-small event count into liquidity/attention buckets shrinks per-cell sample size further.
15. **Liquidity/execution considerations**: this track's entire subject matter *is* liquidity/execution considerations — uniquely, it doesn't need a separate section for this the way the other four do.
16. **Expected observation count**: bounded above by whichever host event track it's paired with — currently that means single digits at best (H-B's 2, H-C's 4, H-019's own 2 executable), since no track currently offers enough events to support meaningful sub-bucketing.
17. **Economically material?**: the state-dependence *hypothesis* is a legitimate, well-established idea in the broader literature (illiquidity/attention-driven underreaction is a real, studied phenomenon) — materiality of any specific finding depends entirely on the host event's own materiality.
18. **Plausibly underprocessed by NGX?**: plausible in principle for the same reason H-B/H-C are (small, illiquid, low-attention names) — again untestable without a host event series large enough to split into states.
19. **Independent of H-011/H-019?**: partially — the state variables (size, liquidity, momentum) **overlap directly with H-011's own construction inputs** (`size_scores()` uses market cap; H-011's own prereg explicitly disclosed liquidity/ADTV as "HIGH correlation by construction" with size) and with H-016's rejected liquidity factor. An H-E interaction built on a size or liquidity state variable would need the same common-cause confound discipline already flagged for H-019 (§14G) — arguably more acutely, since the state variable here is even more directly one of H-011's own inputs, not merely correlated with it.
20. **Decision: CONDITIONAL, but structurally dependent, not independently gateable.** H-E cannot be meaningfully advanced on its own — it is an analysis layer to apply to whichever of H-A/B/C/D eventually clears a coverage gate, not a standalone research track. Its own inputs (price/volume state variables) are ready today; its subject (events to interact with) is not. Recommend holding H-E as a planned second pass on top of H-B specifically (see ranking below), not as a parallel independent effort.

## Single strongest candidate: **H-B — Regulatory Event × Liquidity/Attention**

Not because it currently has the most data — it has the least (2 independent events). It ranks first because it is the only track that is simultaneously: **PIT-clean by construction** (no figure-integrity problem the way H-A has), **explicitly excluded from H-019** rather than redundant with it (the opposite of H-C's problem), **conceptually distinct from H-011's inputs** (the opposite of H-E's problem), and **has a well-defined, bounded, already-proven next step** (the opposite of H-D's problem, which needs a fundamentally new acquisition design before it can even start). H-C's capital_raise coverage is larger in raw count, but inherits the exact redundancy objection that already excluded that event class from H-019 — more data does not fix a structural independence problem.

**Recommended next step, not authorized to begin automatically**: a Stage-11-shaped bounded pilot scoped specifically to `regulatory_action`-type disclosures across the H-011 universe (and a modest, explicitly-bounded expansion beyond it if the 20-name universe proves too thin even for a pilot) — build a frozen, §14C-style direction rule *before* gathering more data (not after, to avoid exactly the hindsight risk the standing rules prohibit), then re-assess coverage against the same gates Stage 13 applied to the news-event track before any hypothesis is registered. H-E should be held as a designed follow-on analysis layer for H-B specifically once H-B's own coverage is large enough to support state-based sub-bucketing — not pursued in parallel as its own track.
