# H-017 — Dividend Payer-Status: Rejected

*2026-08-04. Full pre-registered evaluation complete. Pre-registration:
`docs/PREREG_H-017_dividend_payer_status.md` (frozen before any code was
written or any result viewed). Registered `untested` in
`data/registry.sqlite` before implementation; resolved `rejected` below
with a written conclusion, per the platform's standing ledger discipline.*

---

## Verdict

**REJECTED.** Dividend payer-status does not carry an independent
return premium on NGX at whole-IRU breadth, in the pre-registered
long-payers-vs-EW-IRU form. This is a clean, non-marginal rejection —
multiple independent rejection triggers fired simultaneously, none of
them close to the pre-registered threshold.

**Classification (per PREREG Section 8.3's four-category decision
rule): Null result.** This is not a construct-validity failure
explained by Size or Liquidity — the mandatory orthogonality check
(Section 4 below) found the base effect was never positive to begin
with, and the correlation/bucket evidence independently confirms there
is no effect hiding inside either characteristic either.

---

## 1. What was tested

Long ALL IRU-eligible active dividend payers (binary payer/non-payer
flag, PIT-safe from the trailing 12 months' worth of DOL ex-div
closures — `data/reference/exdiv_closure_calendar.csv`, real DOL-derived
data), equal-weighted, quarterly rebalance base cell, vs. EW-IRU. Full
H-011-grade Phase 4 gauntlet: 2×2 stability grid (rebalance ×
payer-recency window), Holm/BH correction, persistence-preserving
placebo (100 iterations), walk-forward across three regimes with an
untouched final-OOS window, standard failure-condition checks, HAC
(Newey-West) supplementary inference, Deflated Sharpe Ratio against the
existing real-rf trial pool, and the mandatory Section 8 orthogonality
assessment against Size and Liquidity.

### 2.2 Multiple-testing correction

0/4 cells significant at raw p<0.05; 0 survive Holm; 0 survive
Benjamini-Hochberg (min Holm-adjusted p = 0.450).

### 2.3 Placebo

Real Sharpe 1.692 vs. placebo mean 1.624 (placebo p95 = 1.888, placebo
max = 1.973) → **placebo p = 0.366**, decisively above the 0.05 bar. The
real result sits inside the placebo distribution's normal range, not
even near its upper tail.

### 2.4 HAC (Newey-West) inference

t = -1.061, p = 0.289 (7-lag automatic Newey-West rule). Consistent
with every other test — no significance.

### 2.5 Walk-forward

| Regime | Stage | Excess (ann.) |
|---|---|---:|
| pre_float (2016-2023) | development | -3.4% |
| float_shock (2023-2024) | development | +7.0% |
| **oos_2025_26 (untouched final OOS)** | **final_oos** | **-12.2%** |

The single positive regime (float_shock) accounts for 100% of positive
excess across the three regimes — `single_regime_dependency` fired
(limit 80%). The untouched final-OOS window, evaluated exactly once
per the platform's walk-forward discipline, shows the worst excess of
any regime tested. (Note: the mechanical `oos_performance_collapses`
failure check, which compares absolute strategy Sharpe retention, did
not fire — both IS and OOS *absolute* Sharpes were high, because 2025-26
NGX nominal returns were high market-wide during a currency-devaluation
period, affecting the benchmark equally. The economically relevant
number is EXCESS return, which is unambiguously negative in the final
OOS at -12.2% — this nuance is stated explicitly here rather than
letting the non-triggered mechanical check imply a cleaner result than
the excess-return figures actually show.)

### 2.6 Cost/gross decomposition

**Gross excess: +0.02%. Net excess: -3.58%.** The underlying gross
signal is essentially zero — this is not a case of a real gross effect
being eliminated by transaction costs (the pattern seen in some prior
rejections, e.g. H-001/H-007); there was barely any gross signal to
begin with.

### 2.7 Deflated Sharpe Ratio

DSR = 0.018 against the existing 11-hypothesis real-rf trial pool

0.082 — **higher than what H-017 actually achieved.** Even a
purely-lucky best-of-the-pool result would be expected to look better
than this.

### 2.8 Confidence rating

**Low (4/12)** — per `reports/IC_memo_H-017_h017_payer_status_2026-08-04.md`
(auto-generated by the platform's confidence-rating pipeline): data

### 2.9 Capacity

Median per-rebalance capacity ₦460.75M, well above the H-011-style

## 5. Reconciliation with prior findings

This result is directionally consistent with, and adds a new data
point to, the platform's broader pattern: **the technical/
characteristic-based factor space at whole-IRU breadth continues to
show a low hit rate** (1 of 17 hypotheses now confirmed). Unlike
H-016 (Liquidity), which tested a continuous, ranked characteristic in
both directions, H-017 tested a coarse binary flag — Section 15 (L1) of
the pre-registration explicitly flagged in advance that this test
structurally cannot distinguish "no payer effect" from "an effect that
exists only at the yield-magnitude level, which this binary flag cannot
see." **This distinction is repeated here as required**: this result
rejects the payer-STATUS characteristic specifically; it says nothing
about whether dividend YIELD magnitude would behave differently — that
question remains closed on this platform for data-availability reasons
(the DOL EPS/P.E. parser's own twice-documented failure), not reopened
or contradicted by this result.
